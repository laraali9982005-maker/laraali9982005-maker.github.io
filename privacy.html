The Business Lens

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

This policy covers Business Lens Uploader (the "App"), the private publishing tool used by the YouTube channel The Business Lens. It explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and how you can revoke access.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of The Business Lens to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers or other YouTube users.

What the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests these YouTube permissions:

  • youtube.upload (manage your YouTube videos): to upload video files to the channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl (see, edit and delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions): to set a video's thumbnail and upload its captions, and to confirm which channel it is publishing to.

The App does not read or change ratings or comments, does not delete videos, and does not access any Google data beyond what is needed for these tasks.

How data is used

  • The App uses the access granted only to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, tags and captions, and to read the channel's name and ID.
  • Videos are always uploaded as private. The channel owner reviews and publishes each one.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and security

The App stores one OAuth refresh token in a private, access-restricted file in the channel owner's own workspace, so uploads can run without signing in each time. No passwords are stored. YouTube data returned by the API (such as a video ID) is kept only as long as needed to complete an upload.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored token immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
